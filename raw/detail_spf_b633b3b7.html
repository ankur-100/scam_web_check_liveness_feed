Advisory: Beware of Fake Singapore Police Force Websites and Messages

drbypass-proxy.pages.dev

The Singapore Police Force (SPF) has observed a trend of scam websites and messages impersonating official SPF communications. These fraudulent sites aim to trick the public into believing they are interacting with the police, often to solicit personal information or money.

How to recognise these scams:

  • Unusual website addresses: Official SPF websites always end with .gov.sg. Be wary of links that use different endings (e.g., .pages.dev, .com, .net) or contain suspicious characters.
  • Requests for sensitive information: SPF will never ask for your banking details, Singpass password, or one-time passwords (OTPs) through unofficial channels or suspicious links.

What to do if you encounter such a scam or have been affected: If you receive a suspicious message or encounter a website claiming to be SPF, do not click on any links, provide personal details, or make any payments. If you have already done so, immediately stop all communication with the scammers, secure your bank accounts and digital profiles, and make a police report. Inform your family and friends about the scam to help them stay vigilant.

Unsure if something is a scam? Check by calling the 24/7 ScamShield Helpline at 1799.
