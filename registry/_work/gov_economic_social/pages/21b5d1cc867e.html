Scam Advisory

We would like to advise the public to stay vigilant against scams from individuals or entities posing as EnterpriseSG.

Be vigilant against scams

  • Be cautious of unsolicited phone calls, emails, or text messages. Scammers often use these methods to obtain personal information or payment.
  • Be alert when opening text messages or emails from senders you do not recognise. If in doubt, do not click on any links or attachments within the message.
  • Be wary of offers that seem too good to be true and exercise discretion when being approached with them.

Latest scam advisories

  • 4 Jul 2025 - Unauthorised use of the SEEDS Capital name and branding

    We are aware of ongoing unauthorised activities involving the misuse of the SEEDS Capital name, branding and reputation by a group operating primarily in China.

    These activities may be taking place on chat platforms or websites to promote events and community membership recruitment with financial incentive schemes.

    These activities are in no way affiliated with SEEDS Capital, Enterprise Singapore (EnterpriseSG), Singapore Economic Development Board (EDB) or SG Growth Capital. SEEDS Capital does not operate in China and SEEDS Capital officers will never offer financial incentives for participation in their programmes.

    If you have been contacted or come across suspicious materials bearing SEEDS Capital name or logo, we encourage you to exercise caution and report the incident at infohq@sggc.sg.

  • 2 Jul 2024 – Impersonation of EnterpriseSG regarding Singapore Accreditation Council Awards

    We are aware of recent phishing emails impersonating EnterpriseSG personnel, with fraudulent attachments on Singapore Accreditation Council Awards. Please do not reply, click on any link(s), open any attachments, or make any payment to third-party entities without first verifying the authenticity and legitimacy of such emails. If in doubt, please email sac@enterprisesg.gov.sg for assistance.

  • 27 Oct 2023 – Impersonation of EnterpriseSG regarding approval of EDG

    If you receive an email from "services@enterprisesingapore.com" requesting you to preview a document related to your Enterprise Development Grant, do not click the link and delete the email. The email is not sent by EnterpriseSG. 

  • 31 Jul 2024 - Phishing regarding Enterprise Financing Scheme 

    We are aware of a phishing site “noblesingaporeholdltd.com” which appears to offer enterprise loans under the Enterprise Financing Scheme and claims that it will share the loan default risk in the event of enterprise insolvency with the Participating Financial Institutions.

    NobleSingaporeHoldLtd and NobleHoldingssg are not Participating Financial Institutions of the Enterprise Financing Scheme, which is administered by Enterprise Singapore. Enterprise Singapore does not work with nor endorse NobleSingaporeHoldLtd and NobleHoldingssg.

  • 21 Sep 2023 – Misrepresentation of agency

    If you receive an email from "hello@savetech.com.sg" claiming to represent EnterpriseSG to raise awareness of government grants, do not respond and delete it. SaveTech is not a pre-approved Productivity Solutions Grant vendor nor a partner we work with.
