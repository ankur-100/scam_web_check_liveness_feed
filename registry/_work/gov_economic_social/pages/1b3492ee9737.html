Beware of scams impersonating NUH

2024/06/21

We would like to remind our patients, partners and members of public who use our services to stay alert and vigilant in view of impersonation calls, SMSes and phishing scams. Here are some steps to protect yourself against fraud:

  • Do not answer unsolicited calls or reply to unknown SMSes or emails. 
  • Do not divulge sensitive information or login credentials to anyone. 
  • Key in login credentials or personal information only on the OneNUHS App or our official websites. 
  • Ensure that you have entered the correct website address and do not click on links from unsolicited SMSes and/or emails to get to our official websites.
  • If you're uncertain about the authenticity of the calls, SMSes, or emails, contact us at 6908-2222 or contactus@nuhs.edu.sg.

To learn more about scams, visit www.scamalert.sg or call the Anti-Scam Helpline at 1800-722-6688.
