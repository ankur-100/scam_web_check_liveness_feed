Security advisory: Beware of phishing scams

There has been an increase in phishing scams, where scammers impersonate organisations to trick customers into sharing their personal information and passwords.

Here are some ways to protect yourself from these scams:

  • Do not reveal your credit card numbers, passwords or One-Time Passwords (OTP) over any email, text messages, SMS or phone call.
    • We will not request such information from you over a message.
  • Do not scan any PayNow or SingPass QR codes from unknown sources.
  • Do verify links in messages.
    • Links should bring you to "nparks.gov.sg" or ".gov.sg" websites.
  • Do check for the LOCK icon beside the URL in the address bar.

Contact us immediately should you receive any suspicious email, SMS or phone call.
