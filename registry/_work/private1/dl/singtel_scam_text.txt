<ux-heading-text> How to protect yourself
<ux-heading-text> Ensure password safety 
 Practise good password hygiene habits to better protect your data and stay safe online. 
 What you can do 
 Create a password that is a mix of alphabets and numbers, upper and lowercase characters and symbols. 
 Use a password manager app that stores your passwords securely. 
 Use Multi-Factor Authentication which incorporates two or more pieces of information to verify your identity. 
 Never recycle or share your passwords.
<ux-heading-text> Verify your source 
 Scammers trick users into giving away sensitive information by pretending to be trustworthy. 
 What you can do 
 Look out for [HREF:https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=woEEgXEToNU&t=1s#xd_co_f=NjFiODYwZjYtZTI5NS00ZWY0LWIwMGYtMzJiMWM1NTI1ODdj~] impersonation cases where scammers pretend to be working for Singtel or law enforcement agencies. 
 Stay away from [HREF:https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w-i98DjHVjw&t=1s] deals that are too good to be true . 
 Do not click on any links or download any content in email and text messages from unknown sources and delete these immediately. 
 When in doubt, call us at 1688.
