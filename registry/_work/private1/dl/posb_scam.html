POSB Scam Defence​

This is our multi-layered approach to protecting your money. Advanced technology and dedicated teams work around the clock to safeguard your money, while we equip you with the tools and knowledge to protect yourself.
Scam awareness24/7​ protection
Scam education in the community​We make scam awareness accessible through community outreach, branch activities and neighbourhood events. From the POSB Your Neighbourhood Getai series to digital literacy and anti-scam workshops conducted with IMDA, we help Singaporeans recognise threats and stay safe.​
Specialist Anti-Scam TeamMade up of fraud prevention specialists trained to spot the early signs of scam activity, this dedicated unit reviews high-risk transactions flagged by our fraud monitoring systems and works to protect customers from scams.
Frontline staff on alertOur branch and customer service teams undergo regular training to recognise warning signs of scams. They watch for common red flags and if something seems off, they’ll pause the transaction and ask clarifying questions —helping to protect your money before any funds leave your account.
24/7 Fraud Monitoring SystemsOur advanced surveillance systems run round-the-clock to detect unusual activity. If something suspicious is flagged, we’ll contact you and may temporarily block transactions to keep your funds safe.
