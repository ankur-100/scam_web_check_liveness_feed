If you receive a message claiming you’ve won a prize from SBS Transit, please delete it immediately as it’s not from us.

A fake SBS Transit app has also been found on parallel app stores. Only download or update our app through the official Apple App Store or Google Play Store.

To report scams, call the ScamShield helpline at 1799.
