ComfortDelGro Taxi, Your Preferred Taxi Booking Company In Singapore

Following the merger of two successfully listed transport companies – Comfort Group and Delgro Corporation in 2003, Comfort Transportation and CityCab became members of the ComfortDelGro Group. ComfortDelGro Taxi, as it’s known today, is a leading taxi company in Singapore that provides affordable and fuss-free point-to-point services – be it street hail or booking – across Singapore. With a wealth of experience from managing a fleet of 10,000 taxis, to operating both booking and street hailing services, we are more than equipped to train anyone who likes the flexibility that taxi-driving brings become a cabby.
