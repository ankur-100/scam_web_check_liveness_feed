UOB Stash Savings Streak Challenge Promotion (1 October 2026 to 31 December 2026)

The UOB Stash Savings Streak Challenge Promotion rewards eligible customers who consistently grow and maintain their Stash Account savings over consecutive calendar months. Customers who maintain a qualifying Savings Streak may receive cash credits of up to S$195.

To qualify for this Promotion, you must start a Savings Streak in October 2026, November 2026 or December 2026 by maintaining an end-of-month balance of at least S$50,000 in your Stash Account.

A Savings Streak is achieved when, for each consecutive qualifying calendar month, all of the following conditions are met:

  • An end-of-month balance of at least S$50,000 is maintained;
  • Total funds credited into the account during the month amount to at least S$1,000; and
  • No withdrawals, outward fund transfers or other debit transactions that reduce the account balance are made.

Eligible customers may receive a total cash credit of up to S$195 based on the number of Savings Streak maintained:

  • First 3 consecutive qualifying months: S$20 cash credit each month
  • Next 3 consecutive qualifying months: S$45 cash credit each month

A Savings Streak will be considered broken if the customer:

  • fails to maintain the required end-of-month balance of S$50,000;
  • credits less than S$1,000 into the Stash Account during any calendar month; or
  • makes any withdrawal, outward funds transfer or debit transaction that reduces the account balance.

For the purposes of this Promotion, withdrawals include all GIRO deductions and any fees and charges debited from the Stash Account.

This Promotion is open to primary UOB Stash Account holders who are tagged under the Personal Banking, Wealth Banking or Privilege Banking segment. Subject to qualifying criteria. Full terms and conditions apply and can be accessed here.
