Safer ways to verify it’s you

We’re always strengthening our security to stay ahead of evolving threats, with modern safeguards that help protect your account from phishing and scams.

Your passkey only works with official Singpass logins, helping protect you from fake websites and phishing.

Singpass Face Verification confirms it’s really you by scanning your face in real time and matching it against government records.

Know when it’s really Singpass

Take a moment to confirm where you’re logging in and which service is requesting access

On the web

Look for singpass.gov.sg and the lock icon in the address bar before you continue.

In the Singpass app

Check the service name shown in the app before you continue.

Spot suspicious SMSes, emails or calls

Singpass will never send you links or QR codes via SMS or WhatsApp.

Ways to keep your account safer

  • Keep your login details private

    Never share your Singpass password, passcode, or one-time passwords (OTPs) with anyone.

  • Log out when you’re done on desktop

    Especially when using a shared or public device.

  • Use only your own biometrics

    Set up only with your fingerprint or face on your device.

  • Keep your device up to date

    Install the latest software and security updates from official sources.
