Advisory on Scams

  •  Fake Job Advertisements

  •  Email/ Phishing Scams

  •  Cash on Delivery (COD) Scams

 

What are Fake Job Advertisements

They are unauthorised job advertisements usually circulated on messaging platforms and social media platforms that misuse our company name, logo, job listings and outdated contact details.

 

How to Recognise Fake Job Advertisements

All legitimate J&T Express Singapore job openings are published only through the following official channels:
         •        JobStreet
         •        FastJobs
         •        MyCareersFuture
         •        J&T Express Singapore’s corporate website
         •        Our official social media platforms

 

How to Protect Yourself from Fake Job Advertisements

J&T Express Singapore will never request payments, personal banking details, or direct applicants to report to unofficial locations as part of our hiring process.
Members of the public are advised to remain vigilant and disregard any suspicious job offers. If you receive recruitment messages claiming to represent J&T Express Singapore, please do not respond or share personal information, and verify the opportunity through our official channels, including Facebook, Instagram or cs.sg@jtexpress.sg.

 

 

What are Email Scams

Scammers use emails to trick you into giving them your personal information or credentials.

 

How to Recognise Phishing Emails

  • Phishing emails that look like they’re from J&T Express
  • Phishing emails often tell a story to trick you into clicking on a link eg. informing you that your package is waiting to be delivered and you have to pay a certain amount by clicking on a link they provided

 

How to Protect Yourself from Phishing Emails

  • Protect your computer or mobile by using security software
  • Protect your accounts by using multi-factor authentication
  • Always countercheck the sender’s email address, our official email ends with @jtexpress.sg.
  • Never click on any links if the countercheck is not done
  • Links that look like they’re from J&T Express

 

What to Do When You Receive Phishing Emails

If you encounter a suspicious delivery, email and/or payment request, reject immediately and report it to cs.sg@jtexpress.sg.

 

 

What Are Cash On Delivery (COD) Scams 

Cash on delivery scams targets consumers who demand for cash for deliveries that:
1) They did not make an order for; or
2) Did not match their expected purchases.

 

With the recent rise in transactions on E-commerce websites, these scams are becoming more prevalent.
Scammers have been more creative in terms of luring consumers to fall for these scams. These scammers usually:

  • Pose as a seller on marketplaces and send you parcels that you have not made and order for; or
  • Create fake social media accounts to lure consumers to make an order.

Most of the scammers then send their COD parcels over to freight forwarders, who then assign the parcels to J&T Express.

As such, we do not have direct contact with these scammers.

 

How Does COD Scams Work
Scammers send their parcels over to freight forwarders company. These companies then consolidate all of the parcels from various suppliers and send them to logistics company like J&T Express to handle their last-mile deliveries.

J&T Express then sends these parcels to your doorstep and collects cash on delivery on behalf of the freight forwarders. Once cash is collected, the cash is remitted to the forwarders who then remit the cash over to the senders of the parcel.

Later, receivers of the parcel realise that the item they had received is neither the item they had purchase nor an item of the purchased value.

 

Incorrect Cash On Delivery Request
There can be situations where sellers may have incorrectly chosen the Cash On Delivery option while shipping out their product even though payment had already been made.

During these instances, J&T Express will escalate this issue with the shipper to work out an appropriate solution. You will be kept closely updated with the process.

 

Our Fight Against COD Scams
In order to prevent these scams from taking place, J&T Express constantly work closely with freight forwarders and our partners to identify and blacklist scammers from our services.

 

Here Are Ways For You To Protect Yourself From COD Scams

  • DO NOT Pay For Any Parcels That You Have Not Bought
    • If you encounter a suspicious delivery and/or payment request, reject immediately and report the delivery details to cs.sg@jtexpress.sg.

  • Inform Members That Are Receiving The Parcel For You:
    • If others are helping you to receive a parcel that requires COD, let them know what it is, when to expect it and how much to pay.

  • Don't Be Afraid To Reject It:
    • Always check the item description and delivery details. If they look suspicious, reject It!

  • Research Before Purchasing:
    • Check the ratings and the customer reviews of the seller before purchasing.
  • If It Sounds Too Good To Be True, It Probably Isn't What You Are Expecting:
    • If an item is priced at a too good of a price, its most probably not the item you would be expecting.

Other Frequently Asked Questions

1. Why is J&T Express accepting COD as an option?
COD remains an option as we have consumers who still prefer to pay when they receive their parcel as they might not have access to online payments.

2. Why can't the parcel recipients open the parcels before paying for them?
We are bounded by confidentiality contracts agreed with our partners and forwarders to protect all the contents and in the parcel till payment is received. 


3. Why can't J&T Express sieve out scam parcels?
We do not open any parcels and deliver only to the address specified by our shipping partner.
