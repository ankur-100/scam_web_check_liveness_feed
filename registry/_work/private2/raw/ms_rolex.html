Fake Mothership account promotes bogus S$139 Rolex watches

Scam.

 Mothership

August 21, 2026, 06:06 PM

Image from Mothership reader

A fake Mothership account is promoting watch discounts.

Here is the one that was going around last year.

Mothership was alerted by some readers to a new iteration of this, but it also involves watches at a heavily discounted rate.

We are not offering any discounts on watches.

This particular scam implies we have S$139 deals on Rolexes. We do not.

While the page appears to have been taken down, Mothership will continue to work with the relevant authorities to report and remove any other impersonation accounts.

Members of the public should verify the authenticity of pages and links claiming to be affiliated with us before making any purchases.
