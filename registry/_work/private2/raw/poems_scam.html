      Stay Safe When Investing Online – A Message from POEMS by PhillipCapital

      At POEMS, your security and peace of mind matter to us. Protect yourself by staying alert and following these best practices:

      1. Be Cautious of Unverified Tips

        Avoid acting on stock tips or investment recommendations from unofficial sources such as social media or messaging apps.

      2. Secure Your Messaging Apps

        Adjust your privacy settings to prevent unknown parties from adding you to unsolicited investment-related chat groups.

        1. Whatsapp
        2. Telegram

      3. Verify Official Promotions

        Always check our official website at www.poems.com.sg, mobile app, or our verified social media channels (Facebook, Instagram) for updates on promotions and events.

      4. Enable 2FA for Account Protection

        Activate 2-Factor Authentication (2FA) on your POEMS account to add an extra layer of security and safeguard against unauthorised access.

      5. Research Before You Invest

        Understand the risks and do your due diligence before investing in any financial product.

      6. Check Credentials

        Verify the legitimacy of any company or representative through trusted sources like:

        1. MAS Financial Institutions Directory
        2. Register of Representatives
        3. MAS Investor Alert List

      Your financial safety is our top priority. When in doubt, contact us directly at talktophillip@phillip.com.sg or 653 11555.
